A typeface for small, readable FDM labels. At the reference size, nominal strokes are two extrusion widths and clear gaps are at least two extrusion widths. Joins can be thicker; check your own sliced preview.

Size it to your line width

The font is drawn in line widths (w). Set it from the line width your slicer uses for the text. Increasing the size widens strokes and gaps and may add extrusion paths. Below the reference size, minimum widths are less than two extrusion widths.
