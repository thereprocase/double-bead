Fillaprint v0.1.2 is a beta update of the typeface for small FDM-printed labels. We are fine-tuning the letterforms, spacing, and print behavior for release, with an eventual Nerd Fonts launch as a future target. This is not an official Nerd Fonts release.

Download **Fillaprint-0.1.2-beta.zip** for all three families, installation notes, and the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Internal OpenType version: **0.102**.

What changed since v0.1.1:

- **v and z are sharper**, with much less solid fill where their strokes meet. v's arms meet in a plain round join at a wider angle (v is 1.5w wider); z has a steeper 61° diagonal with mitred corners. Both were chosen by a reviewer from five candidates each, shown on the [Fillaprint page](https://thereprocase.github.io/fillaprint/#vz-candidates).
- **V and Z follow**: V has the same round point (now 9w wide, like N and O); Z has mitred corners.
- **Fillaprint Mono** uses the same v and z.
- Spacing and kerning around these letters changed, so text containing them can reflow.

[Try the font, calculate a print size, and view specimens](https://thereprocase.github.io/fillaprint/).

Nominal strokes and minimum clear gaps use two extrusion widths. Check your own sliced preview. The fonts were rebuilt from the tagged sources with the pinned dependencies; this release does not claim new physical-print validation.

Replace installed beta fonts and restart your design application when updating. Keep the v0.1.1 ZIP with projects that need its exact layout.
