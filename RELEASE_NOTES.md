Fillaprint v0.1.3 is a beta update of the typeface for small FDM-printed labels. We are fine-tuning the letterforms, spacing, and print behavior for release, with an eventual Nerd Fonts launch as a future target. This is not an official Nerd Fonts release.

Download **Fillaprint-0.1.3-beta.zip** for all three families, installation notes, and the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Internal OpenType version: **0.103**.

What changed since v0.1.2:

- **Capital V is wider**: 10w of ink (was 9w), as wide as M and W, with the same round point. The wider angle leaves a little less fill in the point.
- **Capital Z has longer top and bottom bars**: each now runs 0.5w past the opposite mitred corner (Z is 1w wider). The diagonal and corners are unchanged.
- Both were chosen by a reviewer from four options each, shown on the [Fillaprint page](https://thereprocase.github.io/fillaprint/#vz-capital-candidates).
- **Fillaprint Mono** uses the same V and Z (both fit the 12w cell).
- Spacing and kerning around V and Z changed, so text containing them can reflow.

[Try the font, calculate a print size, and view specimens](https://thereprocase.github.io/fillaprint/).

Nominal strokes and minimum clear gaps use two extrusion widths. Check your own sliced preview. The fonts were rebuilt from the tagged sources with the pinned dependencies; this release does not claim new physical-print validation.

Replace installed beta fonts and restart your design application when updating. Keep the v0.1.2 ZIP with projects that need its exact layout.
